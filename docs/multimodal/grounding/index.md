## Connection to Previous Topics

Grounding is the natural continuation of video understanding: Qwen2.5-VL's second-level event
localization and timestamped grounding point directly at the question of how models connect
text to **time spans**, not just static regions. The topic spans:

- **Phrase/region grounding**: referring expression segmentation, phrase grounding in images
- **Temporal grounding**: moment retrieval, video grounding, timestamped localization
- **Grounded generation**: outputs that reference specific regions or moments
- **Evaluation**: how grounding quality is measured across image and video domains
